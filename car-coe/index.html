How the 10-year figure is built

Upfront: the vehicle price plus the COE premium. The COE field pulls live bidding results from data.gov.sg, falling back to the September 2026 first-bidding snapshot if the feed is unavailable.

Financing: your downpayment, loan tenure and flat interest rate set the monthly instalment, counted across the full loan tenure.

Running costs across one 10-year COE cycle: insurance, road tax (which differs for petrol and electric), fuel or energy from your monthly distance and consumption, maintenance, plus parking and ERP.

Excluded: resale value, depreciation and the PARF rebate — this is what the car costs you, not what you get back.

Common questions

What does COE cost right now?

The calculator uses live COE bidding results from data.gov.sg, falling back to the September 2026 first-bidding snapshot if the feed is unavailable.

Are electric cars cheaper to run in Singapore?

They cost more to insure (about S$1,650 a year vs S$1,400 for petrol) but less to maintain (about S$80 a month vs S$120). Toggle the fuel type above to compare.

What does the 10-year figure include?

The upfront payment plus instalments over your loan tenure and running costs across one COE cycle. It excludes resale value, depreciation and PARF.

Does the 10-year figure include renewing my COE?

No — it covers a single 10-year COE cycle. Renewing the COE after ten years, or buying a replacement car, is not modelled.

Are dealer discounts or trade-in values included?

No. Enter the price you actually pay after discounts; the figure does not subtract anything further and adds no trade-in value — what you see is the cost of owning, not of selling.
