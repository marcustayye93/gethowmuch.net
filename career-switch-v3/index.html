Pay bands: Occupational Wage Survey 2025 (reference month June 2025, released 30 June 2026). Source: MRSD, MOM. Full-time resident employees in private establishments with at least 25 staff. Figures are monthly, before employee CPF deduction, and exclude bonuses. List ranges show gross P25 to P75; open any occupation for the full three-number band.
This is the survey's frame, not every job in Singapore. It leaves out the public sector, firms with fewer than 25 staff, the self-employed, part-time work, and any occupation the survey did not publish. Lower-paying occupations are listed alongside higher-paying ones, in the survey's own order.

About these numbers

The list shows the gross P25 to P75 range for each occupation. Opening an occupation shows the full band: the 25th percentile, median, and 75th percentile of monthly basic and gross wages reported by employers for June 2025. Basic wage excludes overtime, commissions and allowances. Gross wage includes them. Both exclude bonuses, which matter more in jobs like brokers, dealers and senior management, so those bands read lower than total pay. The survey covers private-sector establishments with at least 25 employees. Occupations are coded from employer-supplied job titles, so some rows mix senior and junior holders of the same title. Figures describe incumbents in June 2025. They are a market reference, not a prediction of any individual's pay, and the 25th percentile is not starting pay.

Source: MRSD, MOM, Occupational Wages Tables 2025. Reskill paths: GetHowMuch career index research, 2026 (editorial). Demand notes are editorial and dated, never part of any pay figure.
