Privacy Policy

Effective 12 September 2026

The short version: GetHowMuch has no accounts and no logins. Everything you type into a calculator stays in your browser — it is never sent to us.

Calculator inputs

All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your salary, savings, age and other inputs are processed by JavaScript on your device and never transmitted to our servers. We cannot see, store or sell what you type.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic (for example, how many people visit each calculator). It is designed to measure visits without cookies and without collecting personal data — no names, no email addresses, no cross-site tracking.

Advertising

We plan to show ads served by Google AdSense to keep the calculators free — there are no ads on the site yet. When ads go live, Google may use cookies to personalise the ads you see. You can learn how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage your ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Affiliate and partner links

Some pages contain affiliate or partner links, always labelled as such. If you click one and sign up with that provider, they may set their own cookies to attribute the referral, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products relevant to the calculator you are using.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The effective date at the top will always show the latest version.
