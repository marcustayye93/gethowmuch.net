Common questions

What is a Year of Assessment (YA)?

Your YA2026 tax is computed on income you earned in 2025. E-filing for YA2026 typically closes in mid-April 2026.

How can I legally pay less income tax in Singapore?

Common moves the optimizer models: CPF cash top-ups, SRS contributions and charitable donations — each reduces your chargeable income. Try them above to see the saving.

Is income tax deducted from my monthly salary?

No. Your employer deducts CPF, not income tax. IRAS bills you after you file each year, and you can pay in one lump sum or up to 12 interest-free GIRO instalments.

How this estimate works

This combines two planning views: employee CPF using rates effective 1 Jan 2026, and a YA2026 income-tax estimate for income earned in 2025. The tax view annualises your monthly gross pay, subtracts employee CPF, earned income relief and any reliefs you enter below, then applies YA2026 progressive rates.

  • YA2026 resident ratesThirteen progressive bands, from 0% to 24%, in force from YA2024 onwards.
  • No YA2026 rebateThe 60% SG60 rebate, capped at S$200, applied to YA2025 only. It is not carried into this estimate.
  • CPF employee contributionUses the age-based employee rate and the S$8,000 monthly Ordinary Wage ceiling from 1 Jan 2026. The annual salary ceiling is S$102,000.
  • Reliefs includedEmployee CPF contributions and earned income relief (S$1,000 below 55, S$6,000 from 55–59, S$8,000 from 60), plus the SRS, CPF top-up, NSman, parent, child and donation amounts you enter.
  • Relief ceilingTotal personal reliefs are capped at S$80,000 per Year of Assessment.
What is not modelled: bonuses, the additional-wage CPF ceiling, non-employment income, life insurance relief, and individual circumstances. CPF and earned-income-relief age tests are not identical; for this simple estimate, the “55 & below” chip uses S$1,000 relief and “Above 55–60” uses S$6,000. At the exact age boundaries, your assessed relief may differ.

Sources: Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (resident rates, rebates and reliefs) and Central Provident Fund Board (2026 employee rates and ceilings). Estimate only, not tax advice.
